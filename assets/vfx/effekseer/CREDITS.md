# Effekseer effects (CC0)

These effects are the Effekseer sample and contributed effects, released under CC0 by their authors, played with the
Effekseer runtime for Godot 4 (MIT, addons/effekseer). Credit as requested by the Effekseer project.

- Effekseer Project, official samples and Effekseer Official Effects (Effekseer01)
- BrokenDesk, MAGICAL x SPIRAL Effects (MAGICALxSPIRAL)
- tktk, tktk Effect and tktk Effects vol.2 (tktk01, tktk02)
- Pierre, Pierre's Effects vol.1 and vol.2 (Pierre01, Pierre02)
- NextSoft, Riemebelta effects (NextSoft01)
- AndrewFM (AndrewFM01)
- Suzuki Katsushi (Suzuki01)
- HATO, Spring has come (HATO01)

Source https://effekseer.github.io/en/contribute.html (Sample Effects, CC-0 License). Converted from .efkproj to .efkefc
with the Effekseer 1.80.7 editor's command line, unchanged.
